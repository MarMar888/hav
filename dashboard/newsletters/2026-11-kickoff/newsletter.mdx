# Welcome to our first newsletter

Hi! My name is Marley, I am the lead for Haav, a student boat team at UW-Madison, competing in PEP27. You have been added to this email newsletter if you have supported, advised, or helped us in any way. Thank you!! Below is the basic overview of the competition, our strategy and our progress.


## The competition constraints

A planing hull carrying a 30 pound added payload over a 2 mile course. Fastest time wins. Must be electric, under 55V, and have positive buoyancy.

## Strategy

- Test early and often
- Don't reinvent the wheel, learn from the coffins (past teams). Fix the bottlenecks they had.
- Data and software can give us a huge leg up, use it to accelerate hardware.

## Our progress
- We made a model boat and are testing it in the water this week! This model boat will serve as our test platform for autonomy, GPS, IMU and communication systems, as well as a place to see basic feedback on hull design.
